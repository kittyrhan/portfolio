About me

Curiosity is where I start.

I’m Ruohan, a product designer at TikTok Search and an MS student in Human Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington.

Outside design, I’m a photographer, vlogger, and storyteller. I love capturing the quiet moments that reveal how people interact with the world—the same curiosity that shapes my design work.

Across systems and stories

My path has moved through interaction design, mobility, educational AI, and search—always grounded in understanding how people make decisions in real contexts.

Understand people before designing for them

I begin by exploring how people think, behave, and decide. Those insights help me frame the right problem before moving into ideas and prototypes. Through iteration and close collaboration with product managers and engineers, I translate complex needs into clear, approachable experiences.

Research grounds the work in real behavior. Systems turn complexity into patterns that can scale across products and edge cases. Storytelling makes the insight clear enough for a team to align, decide, and move forward together.

The details I stop for

Photography trains me to notice atmosphere, gestures, and small interactions—the details that often become the starting point for better design questions.

Let’s make something thoughtful

I’m always happy to talk about AI, search, human behavior, photography, or a question you cannot stop thinking about.
