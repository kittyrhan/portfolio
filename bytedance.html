01Project overview

Gauth serves middle-school, high-school, and college students with instant feedback, step-by-step explanations, and personalized recommendations. The goal was to shorten problem-solving time while supporting independent learning and critical thinking.

02User research

50+ student interviews revealed the gap between getting an answer and understanding it.

I led discovery through product definition, drawing on more than 50 student interviews. We looked at students’ workloads, confidence, and the pressure behind instant help; where they reviewed reasoning or stopped engaging; and what made AI guidance understandable and verifiable.

Following the capture, review, and follow-up loop revealed a recurring tension: efficiency brought students in, but transparency and structure determined whether they kept learning. Students needed evidence they could trust and practice they could return to.

03Problem definition

Two learning problems shaped the product direction.

Two problems shaped the direction. Students often received a correct answer without understanding its logic or evidence. Meanwhile, scattered ACT and SAT practice made it hard to focus on weak areas or track progress by topic.

04Solution design — Smart Source Retrieval

Put the evidence beside the answer

Smart Source Retrieval added multiple layers of verification for humanities subjects. Gauth retrieves academic sources, displays supporting evidence beside the answer, and connects reasoning with historical or conceptual context.

With Smart Source Retrieval, students can open verified academic references and trace an explanation back to its source. Guided breakdowns connect facts to the historical or conceptual context around them.

05Solution design — Concept-based Test Prep

Turn a mistake into the next learning step

Concept-based Test Prep organizes ACT and SAT content around key topics, then provides reflective, step-by-step feedback after each mistake to strengthen comprehension and long-term confidence.

Explaining why an answer is correct encourages understanding rather than memorization. Visible progress and supportive feedback help students keep going when they feel stuck.

06Launch & product outcomes

From interviews to a shipped product

I presented the work across the team and translated the research into product requirements for the next iteration. Following the upgrade, Gauth reported a 10% increase in daily active users, reached 4 million global users, and ranked #1 in Education in the US App Store.

07Reflection

The project taught me how students use educational tools in real learning contexts, and why AI education needs to support reflection and confidence alongside speed. Close collaboration with design, engineering, and product helped turn qualitative insight into shared decisions.
