01Project Overview & The Challenge

TikTok Search brings together videos, LIVE, short dramas, vertical results, and AI-generated answers. The challenge is to make these different formats feel clear and consistent within one search experience.

As a Product Design Intern on the comprehensive search team, I contributed to card patterns, AI answer presentation, Search Feedback, and platform adaptation. I developed proposals with design, product, and engineering, and owned Core Search iOS foldable adaptation end to end.

02Building a Scalable Content System

Different content types carry different metadata and visual weight. I worked on vertical cards, LIVE cards, and LIVE cards within vertical search, focusing on the relationship between shared layout rules and content-specific needs.

Adaptive card height addressed differences in content density. A weaker treatment for non-leading short dramas helped distinguish content priority. Across these projects, my focus was consistent sizing and spacing, with room for metadata, height, and emphasis to adapt.

03Making Search Results Easier to Understand

AI answers needed a clearer relationship between text, images, video, and the space around them. I audited the existing components and explored how answer hierarchy and media placement could make results easier to scan.

Short answers & highlights

My short-answer work focused on selective emphasis and lightweight reveal motion. The collapsed and expanded references show the shift from a quick answer to deeper reading; I refined the highlight treatment and reveal behavior within this experience.

Rich-media answers

I compared horizontal media with a full-width layout. Horizontal scrolling preserved vertical space; full width gave media greater emphasis but occupied more of the feed. I refined container sizing, spacing, hierarchy, and interaction cues, and isolated details such as badge placement for review.

The redesigned AI screens have not launched. Product references and contribution summaries are shared here; confidential final designs are not shown.

04Designing for Real-World Interactions

I owned Core Search iOS foldable adaptation end to end, organizing active search and passive discovery across screen widths. The work covered bottom popups, in-flow media escalation, toast feedback, and secondary pages, with multi-width Figma specifications and a structured PRD for handoff.

Search Feedback

I also contributed to the Search Feedback panel, considering the entry point, panel behavior, and feedback states as part of the wider search interaction system.

Foldable work is still in progress.

05Exploring the Future of AI Search

I compared summary-style AI search with conversational generative AI, looking at when answers appear, how they are structured, how text and media work together, where sources are shown, and how exploration continues.

This provided a lens for the design work: define a clear first layer of the answer, connect media to its meaning, and keep evidence and deeper exploration accessible. The relationship between a quick answer and a follow-up conversation remains a useful question for AI search.

Selected competitor examples and the original research synthesis to be added.

06Outcomes & Reflections

My work spans card patterns and adaptive layouts, short-answer and rich-media proposals, Search Feedback, and AI competitive research. For foldable search, I delivered the scenario framework, multi-width Figma specifications, and a structured PRD.

I learned to connect component behavior, content rules, and platform constraints before refining a screen. Comparing layouts made tradeoffs clearer; working through specifications strengthened my ownership of states, edge cases, and implementation guidance.

AI proposals and foldable work are still in progress. Future review and validation should examine readability, media presentation, and behavior across screen widths. No post-launch metrics are reported here.
